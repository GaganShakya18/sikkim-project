Travel Guidelines & Regulations

Entry Permits

Inner Line Permit (ILP) required for Indian citizens. Protected Area Permit (PAP) needed for restricted areas. Carry valid ID proof at all times.

Environmental Protection

No plastic bags allowed. Maintain cleanliness in monasteries and trekking areas. Follow Leave No Trace principles. Respect wildlife and natural habitats.

Monastery Etiquette

Remove shoes before entering. Dress modestly. No photography inside prayer halls without permission. Maintain silence during prayers and ceremonies.

Trekking Safety

Hire certified guides for high-altitude treks. Carry proper gear and first aid. Inform authorities about trekking plans. Check weather conditions before departure.
