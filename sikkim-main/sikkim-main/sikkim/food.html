Sikkim Food & Cuisine

Authentic Flavors of the Himalayas

Food Preferences

Traditional Thali

Experience the authentic Sikkimese thali featuring dal, rice, seasonal vegetables, and traditional pickles. This complete meal represents the essence of local cuisine with its perfect balance of flavors and nutrition.

Momos

Steamed dumplings filled with vegetables or meat, served with spicy chutney. These Tibetan-influenced delicacies have become synonymous with Sikkim's street food culture and are loved by locals and tourists alike.

Gundruk

Fermented leafy green vegetables that form an essential part of Nepali and Sikkimese cuisine. This nutritious dish is rich in vitamins and provides a unique tangy flavor that complements rice and dal perfectly.

Chhurpi

Traditional yak cheese that can be consumed fresh or dried. This protein-rich delicacy is a staple food in high-altitude regions and represents the pastoral heritage of Sikkim's mountain communities.

Sel Roti

Traditional ring-shaped rice bread made from rice flour, milk, and sugar. This sweet delicacy is prepared during festivals and special occasions, offering a crispy exterior and soft interior.

Phagshapa

Traditional pork curry cooked with radish and dried chilies. This spicy dish is a staple in Sikkimese households and represents the authentic flavors of the region's meat-based cuisine.

Kinema

Fermented soybean curry that's rich in protein and probiotics. This traditional dish is prepared with tomatoes, onions, and local spices, making it a healthy and flavorful vegetarian option.

Dhindo

Traditional porridge made from millet or corn flour. This nutritious staple food is served with vegetables, pickles, or curry, providing essential carbohydrates and energy for mountain life.

Churpee Ningro

Dried yak cheese cooked with fiddlehead ferns. This unique combination offers a perfect blend of protein from cheese and vitamins from wild ferns, representing mountain foraging traditions.

Saelroti

Sweet rice flour donuts prepared during Tihar festival. These golden rings are made with rice flour, milk, and cardamom, offering a delightful sweet treat that's crispy outside and soft inside.

Sukuti

Dried meat jerky made from buffalo or yak meat. This protein-rich preserved food is seasoned with local spices and serves as a long-lasting source of nutrition for high-altitude living.
