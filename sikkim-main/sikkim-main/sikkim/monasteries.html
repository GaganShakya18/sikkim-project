Sikkim Monasteries

Sacred Sanctuaries of the Himalayas

Rumtek Monastery

Also known as the Dharma Chakra Centre, Rumtek is one of Sikkim's largest and most significant monasteries. Built in the 1960s, it serves as the seat of the Karmapa and houses precious relics, thangkas, and statues. The monastery is renowned for its traditional Tibetan architecture and spiritual significance in the Kagyu school of Tibetan Buddhism.

Pemayangtse Monastery

One of the oldest and most important monasteries in Sikkim, Pemayangtse means "perfect sublime lotus." Founded in 1705, it belongs to the Nyingma school of Tibetan Buddhism. The monastery is famous for its seven-tiered wooden sculpture depicting the heavenly palace of Guru Rinpoche and offers breathtaking views of the Kanchenjunga range.

Dubdi Monastery

Established in 1701, Dubdi is considered one of Sikkim's oldest monasteries. Located in Yuksom, it was built by Lhatsun Chempo, one of the three lamas who consecrated the first Chogyal of Sikkim. The monastery, meaning "the retreat," sits atop a hill and is accessible via a scenic trek through dense forests.

Tashiding Monastery

Considered the heart of Sikkim, Tashiding Monastery is revered as a sacred pilgrimage site. Founded in 1641, it holds immense religious significance. Located on a hilltop between the Rathong and Rangeet rivers, it's believed that a glimpse of this monastery cleanses one's sins.

Enchey Monastery

Built in 1909, Enchey Monastery is located on a hilltop in Gangtok. The name "Enchey" means "solitary temple" in the local language. This monastery belongs to the Nyingma order and is famous for its annual Cham dance performed during the Pang Lhabsol festival.

Ralang Monastery

Founded in 1768, Ralang Monastery is one of the most important Kagyu monasteries in Sikkim. Located near Ravangla, it houses ancient murals, scriptures, and religious artifacts. The monastery is known for its peaceful atmosphere and stunning mountain views.

Phensang Monastery

Established in 1721, Phensang Monastery is located in Kabi, North Sikkim. This ancient monastery belongs to the Nyingma school and is known for its historical significance and beautiful architecture. It offers panoramic views of the surrounding valleys and mountains.

Sanga Choeling Monastery

Built in 1697, Sanga Choeling is the second oldest monastery in Sikkim. Located on a ridge above Pelling, it offers spectacular views of the Kanchenjunga range. The monastery houses ancient Buddhist scriptures and is an important pilgrimage site for devotees.
