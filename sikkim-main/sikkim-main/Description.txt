Description	
Background

Sikkim is home to over 200 monasteries, many of which date back to the 17th and 18th centuries. These monasteries are rich in history, architecture, and spiritual significance. While some efforts have been made to preserve murals and digitize rare documents, there is no unified digital platform that offers tourists and researchers immersive access to these cultural treasures.

Proposed Solution

Monastery360: A Digital Heritage Platform for Sikkim’s Monasteries

Key Features:
- Virtual Tours: 360° panoramic views of monastery interiors and surroundings.
- Narrated walkthroughs in multiple languages.
- Interactive Map: Geo-tagged monastery locations with travel routes and nearby attractions.
- Integration with local transport and tourism services.
- Digital Archives: Scanned manuscripts, murals, and historical documents.
- AI-powered search and categorization.
- Smart Audio Guide App: Location-based audio guides using Bluetooth beacons or GPS.
- Offline mode for remote areas.
- Cultural Calendar: Events, festivals, and rituals schedule.
- Booking and participation options for tourists.

Impact

- Boosts tourism by making monasteries more accessible.
- Preserves endangered cultural assets digitally.
- Empowers local communities through participatory archiving.
- Supports educational and spiritual exploration globally.



--Problem Being Solved:

Protecting and promoting Sikkim's monasteries without compromising cultural authenticity and community benefits.

--Why Solve It?

Preserves heritage, enhances tourism responsibly, empowers communities.

--What's Unique?

Integrates AI and community participation in a user-friendly platform.

--Added Value:

Immersive, accessible way to explore, preserve and learn about Sikkim's rich cultural heritage.

--what does it have that others dont???
The proposed platform has several unique features that set it apart from other existing resources:

-Unifies digital resources on Sikkim's monasteries under one platform.
-Leverages AI for intelligent search and categorization of manuscripts.
-Incorporates local monk/nun participation for verification and ongoing updates.
-Combines virtual tours, interactive maps, and offline audio guides all in one mobile app.
-This makes Monastery360 an all-encompassing, accessible, and culturally respectful resource for locals and tourists alike.


-why new things have you done for innovation?
Here are the key innovations in your Monastery360 project that don't exist in current solutions:

1. AI + Community-Powered Cultural Preservation
🧠 AI-Assisted Archives: First to combine OCR (Tesseract.js) and monk-verified metadata tagging for manuscripts/murals, ensuring accuracy + cultural context.
👥 Participatory Archiving: Locals/nuns can upload photos/audio via simple app – crowdsourcing preservation while empowering communities.
2. Hybrid Online/Offline Experience
📶 Smart Offline Mode: Audio guides sync when online, work seamlessly offline via GPS/BLE beacons (unlike WiFi-dependent platforms).
🗺️ Travel-Ready Maps: Downloads routes/attractions when connected – no signal needed in remote Himalayan areas.
3. Immersive Yet Accessible Tech
🖼️ "Budget VR" 360° Tours: Uses lightweight A-Frame library instead of bulky Unity/Unreal, making tours load faster on basic phones.
🔊 Local-Language First: Narrations recorded by native Sikkimese speakers (not AI voices) for authenticity (+ optional English/Hindi).
4. Monetization That Benefits Locals 🔥 (Judges Love This!)
💸 Shared Revenue Model: 20% of premium subscriptions fund monastery maintenance/training – aligning tourism with preservation goals.
